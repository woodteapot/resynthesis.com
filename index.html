01 · The Programme

A study with no end date, by design.

The Resynthesis Programme began as a feasibility memo with eleven signatures and no budget line. It now has a budget line and no signatures, because every signatory has since been regenerated reassigned. Its single stated aim is to reverse the arrow of tissue: to take what has worn, torn or been forgotten and synthesise it back, better than the original and slightly ahead of schedule.

AIM-1

Restore

Rebuild anything that once grew. Cartilage, enamel, the ability to sit cross-legged.

AIM-2

Revise

Rebuild it with fewer flaws than the factory shipped. Version 2 of the human knee.

AIM-3

Remember

Rebuild the things that never grew at all. This aim is discussed only on Thursdays.

02 · The Concordat of Five

Five companies. One table. No minutes.

Resynthesis is underwritten by five pharmaceutical houses that, on paper, compete fiercely and have never met. Their annual reports describe vitamins, eye drops and a promising antacid. Their boards meet once a year, aboard a ship that is always being repainted.

MEMBER I · FOUNDED 1931

Paper Lamp Biologics

Holds the original culture, a sample that has been dividing since before the war and refuses to stop. Keeper of the Ledger.

MEMBER II · FOUNDED 1958

Vellum Crane Pharmaceutical

Officially makes bandages. Unofficially makes the skin that goes under them. Its logo is a crane folded from a single sheet with no cuts.

MEMBER III · FOUNDED 1966

Saltgate Meridian Life Sciences

Supplies the seawater, filtered through nine depths of the trench. Nobody has asked why it must be that water. Nobody is going to.

MEMBER IV · FOUNDED 1987

Ninefold Tide Holdings

A holding company that holds only the other four. Signs every cheque, attends no meeting, has a single employee whose badge reads "Auditor".

MEMBER V · FOUNDED 0000

Hollow Pearl Biotech

Joined the Concordat in a year that has not happened yet. Its paperwork is impeccable. Its address is a number with no street.

04 · Facility

Site Kestrel.

The work is carried out on a retired research platform somewhere in the waters of East Asia, registered to no flag and visible on no chart printed after 1974. Supply boats arrive on the new moon. The platform has seventeen decks above the water and an unconfirmed number below it.

DECK 1

Reception

A single desk, a single plant, and a receptionist who has worked here since 1974 and looks thirty-one.

DECK 9

The Nursery

Rows of warm tanks, faint humming, and a strict rule against naming anything that grows.

DECK −4

The Ledger Room

Every result, written by hand, in an ink that slowly regenerates if you try to erase it.

DECK 00

The Deep Lab

Access requires two keys and an apology.

05 · Findings

Recovered memorandum.

The following page was found folded inside a returned lab coat. It has been reproduced as found.

TO: All deck leads
FROM: Office of the Auditor
RE: Phase III, specimen 7 ("Gerald")

Specimen 7 has once again regenerated its own containment tank. The new tank is better than the old tank. Facilities would like to know where it is getting the glass.

Reminder that the Concordat does not use the word "immortal" in writing. Please use "indefinitely ongoing".

The vending machine on Deck 9 has begun regenerating snacks. This is not an approved study arm. Do not eat the second packet.

The board meets on the fourteenth. The ship is being repainted. Wear something you don't mind getting resynthesised.

06 · Participation

We do not recruit.

There is no application, no form and no mailing list. Participants are selected by a process that is reviewed annually and understood by nobody. If you have been selected you will know, because you will receive a single origami crane with no cuts, and your knees will feel unusually good for a week.

Notice to the curious

Please do not look for Site Kestrel. It is not on the map, it is not in the phone book, and it is very tired of polite letters from people who found it anyway.

Resynthesis is fiction. The Programme, the Concordat, its five companies, Site Kestrel and everyone mentioned here are invented. Any resemblance to real companies, studies, places or people is coincidence.
